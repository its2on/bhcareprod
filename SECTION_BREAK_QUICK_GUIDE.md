## 🔍 **Key Visual Features:**

1. **🟧 Orange Left Border** (6px thick) - Can't miss it!
2. **⬜ Gray Gradient Background** - Stands out from white
3. **≡ Icon** - Visual indicator
4. **🟠 Orange Title** - Matches BHCARE theme
5. **📏 Large Spacing** - 40px gaps above/below
6. **🎨 Shadow** - Adds depth

---

## 🚀 **Try It Now:**

1. **Hard refresh** your browser: `Ctrl + Shift + R`
2. Open your form in Form Builder
3. Click **"Preview Form"**
