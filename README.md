# BHCare

**BHCare** is a web-based Barangay Health Center Constituents’ Records Solution designed for Barangays **158, 159, 160, and 161 of Caloocan City**. The system digitizes patient records, appointment scheduling, notifications, and reporting to improve efficiency, data accuracy, and healthcare service delivery at the community level.

---

## Project Overview

BHCare was developed to address the limitations of paper-based health record systems commonly used in barangay health centers, such as lost records, long waiting times, and difficulty in generating reports. The system provides a secure, centralized platform for health workers and residents to manage healthcare-related activities.

This project was developed as a **Capstone Project** for the **Bachelor of Science in Information Technology** program at **STI College Novaliches**.

---

## Objectives

- Digitize and automate barangay health records management  
- Enable online appointment scheduling for residents  
- Provide automated reminders for check-ups and immunizations  
- Generate monthly and annual health reports  
- Ensure data privacy, integrity, and system security  

---

## Target Users

- **Administrator** – Manages users, permissions, system settings, and audit logs  
- **Doctor** – Conducts consultations, records diagnoses, and generates reports  
- **Nurse** – Manages immunization records, appointments, and patient data  
- **Patient/Resident** – Views personal records, books appointments, and receives notifications  

---

## System Features

### Authentication & Access Control
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)  
- Residency verification using ID upload and OCR  
- Secure login and account approval process  

### Patient Records Management
- Digital storage of personal data, medical history, and immunization records  
- Secure viewing and updating of patient information  

### Appointment Scheduling
- Online booking, modification, and cancellation of appointments  
- Real-time validation to prevent overlapping schedules  

### Notification System
- Automated reminders for appointments, immunizations, and check-ups  
- Notifications via in-app alerts and email  

### Reports & Analytics
- Monthly and annual health reports  
- Graphical summaries of common illnesses and patient trends  
- Exportable reports (PDF)  

### Security & Data Protection
- AES-256 encryption for stored data  
- Encrypted backups and audit trails  
- Input validation and restricted access

## Scope and Limitations

### Scope
- Applicable only to Barangays 158–161 of Caloocan City  
- Supports general consultation, immunization, prenatal, dental, and DOTS programs  

### Limitations
- Requires internet connectivity  
- Dependent on supported devices and operating systems  
- Manual data entry may still be prone to human error

## Evaluation Results

System testing conducted at **Baesa Health Center** produced an **overall weighted mean of 3.80**, indicating that users found the system effective, user-friendly, and secure.
